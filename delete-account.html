Delete your Drone Detect account

Drone Detect (com.dronedetect.app), developed by Bryce Kaplan

Accounts are only used for the optional multi-phone alerts feature. If you never created an account, we hold no data about you. Everything stays on your phone, and uninstalling the app removes it.

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Drone Detect and tap Settings.
  2. Under Multi-phone alerts, tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and all its data are deleted from our servers immediately.

Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the app)

Email aj2qg7hm@mail.grokbot.com with the subject "Delete my Drone Detect account", from the email address you used to sign up.

We verify that the request comes from the account's email address, delete the account within 30 days, and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address, password hash and account ID
  • All phones linked to the account: names, roles, notification tokens, check-in times and quiet hours
  • All alerts (camera, time and confidence score) and daily alert counts

What is kept

Nothing is kept after deletion. Our servers' technical error logs are deleted automatically after 7 days and contain no email addresses, tokens or alert contents.
